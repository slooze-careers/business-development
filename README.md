# 🚀 Slooze take home challenge-business-development!!

## Scenario
You’ve just joined **Slooze** [slooze.xyz], a SaaS startup with strong free-trial signups but slower-than-expected paid conversions.  
Users love the product — but **decision-makers remain skeptical**, and **customer churn is rising**.  
Show us how you’d help **Slooze grow**.

---

## 🧩 Your Challenge – 3 Tasks

### 1️⃣ Convert Free Trials → Paying Users
Propose **two actionable ideas** to boost conversions.  
Think: engagement triggers, personalized follow-ups, or trust builders.  
> Keep it practical and measurable.

---

### 2️⃣ Pitch the Value Proposition
Write a **1-minute pitch** for a **decision-maker** who controls the budget but isn’t the daily user.  
Your goal: **differentiate Slooze**, overcome skepticism, and highlight **ROI**.

---

### 3️⃣ Reduce Churn & Improve Onboarding
List **three ways** to improve onboarding so users quickly see value and stay longer.  
Focus on activation moments, user education, or feedback loops.

---

- **Deadline:** Take your time
